# Presentation scoring

Each criterion is scored 1 to 4. The course is not graded; the scores guide feedback.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity | The finding is not stated. | The finding appears late or is unclear. | The finding is stated first and is mostly clear. | The finding is stated first, in one sentence, and everything supports it. |
| Evidence | No numbers or chart. | Numbers are given but not linked to the finding. | One chart or number clearly supports the finding. | The evidence is checked, simple, and answers the question directly. |
| Recommendation | There is none. | It is vague, like do better. | A clear action. | A clear action, with who and why, linked to the evidence. |
| Delivery | Reads the slides, no structure. | Some structure, but rushed or too long. | Clear structure and on time. | Clear structure, on time, calm pace, numbers said simply. |

## Other scores

**Project (30%), scored 10 / 10 / 10.**
- Question and plan: the question is specific, answerable, and the plan is written.
- Analysis and checked numbers: the steps run, row counts are shown, one number is checked a second way.
- Chart and finding: the chart has a title that states the finding and axis labels, and the finding is clear.

**Cold test (15%), out of 4.** It runs (1). The numbers are right, within 0.01 (1). The names are clear (1). One sentence says what it means (1).

**Self-reflection (15%).** Complete (all six questions), partial, or missing.

**Participation and daily recall (20%).** Does she explain last session in her own words, and does she do the daily recall?
