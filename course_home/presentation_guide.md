# Presentation guide

**Answer first.** Say your finding in the first 30 seconds. Then show the evidence. Then say what to do.

**Three content slides,** plus a cover and a thank-you slide. Use `presentation_template.pptx`.
1. **Question.** One sentence.
2. **Finding.** One chart and one big number.
3. **Recommendation.** What should a manager do?

**Your 5 minutes:** 1 minute question and context. 2 minutes finding and evidence. 1 minute recommendation. 1 minute spare.

**Say numbers simply.** "About 1 in 5 products" is better than "21.9%". Round, and say what it means.

**Do not read the slides.** Look at the person. The slide supports you.

**Language.** Slides are in English. You present in English. If you feel nervous, you may answer questions in Bahasa Indonesia.

**The 2-minute walk-through** (for the question "Walk me through your project"):
1. The question, in one sentence.
2. What data you used, and one cleaning step.
3. The finding, with one number.
4. The recommendation.
5. One limit of your analysis.

**Questions to practise:** Why did you remove those rows? What would you do with more time? How did you check your numbers?
