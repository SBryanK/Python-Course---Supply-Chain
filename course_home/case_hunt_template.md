# Case hunt

Find one real supply chain case: from the news, a company report or a course case. Write one page.

**Case and source:**

1. **The problem.** What went wrong, or what is hard?
2. **Who is affected.** Customers, suppliers, workers, the company?
3. **What data would show it.** Which numbers would you look at?
4. **One question you could answer with data.** Specific, and answerable.
5. **Which tool fits.** Python, SQL or a dashboard? Why?

Handed out in Session 6. Bring it to Session 8 and present it in 2 minutes. Link it to our sales data in one sentence.
