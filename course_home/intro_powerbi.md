# Intro to Power BI (self-study, one page)

Power BI is a tool for dashboards. You click, you do not code. Python prepares and checks the data. Power BI shows it.

## Install
Install **Power BI Desktop** on your Windows laptop (free). Do this before Session 7.

## Build one chart and one card
1. Open Power BI Desktop. Choose **Get data**, then **Text/CSV**.
2. Pick `product_value.csv` from your `work` folder in Google Drive. Click **Load**.
3. **Bar chart:** click the bar chart icon. Drag `stock_code` to the axis and `revenue` to the values.
4. **Card:** click the card icon. Drag `revenue` to it, and set it to show the sum.
5. Save the file. Take a screenshot of your report and send it to your teacher.

## Check
- Does the card show about 1,876,534? That is the total revenue.
- Does your bar chart have a title that says the finding? Add one with the Format pane.
